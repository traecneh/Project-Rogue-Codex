1998–1999
Official lineage

Dransik Classic

The original Dransik establishes the world and community behind the later branches.Read the storyClose story ⌄

The original top-down Dransik emerged in the late 1990s, with an Ultima-inspired world, skills, crafting, and player conflict. Historical accounts use both 1998 and 1999 for its beginnings; this range avoids assigning a single definitive launch day.

This original game became known as Dransik Classic. Its later history runs alongside the newer isometric Dransik, rather than disappearing into it.

2002
Official lineage

The Dransik Split

Classic and the newer isometric Dransik coexist as distinct versions.Read the storyClose story ⌄

Jeff Barrett’s October 2002 guide distinguishes an older-graphics YouCanGame server from the new isometric Dransik New World beta. Their quest and guild systems also differed.

The guide lists New World, Krythan Inception in California, Great Wall in Asia, and Mystery and Magic, an experimental magic-system server. These were server variants of the newer game—not four separate descendants.

The newer branch is also referred to in historical accounts as New World / Primordia / ISO and later becomes Ashen Empires.

Imbuements

Material

Tattered Imbuement

Each tatter is tied to one perk name. Matching tatters are the material used to craft that perk scroll.

Craft

250 Matching Tatters

Spend 250 Matching Tatters in the Craft menu to create one Scroll of Imbuement.

Apply

Epic+ Item

Use the imbuement tool to apply a crafted Scroll of Imbuement to an Epic+ weapon or armor piece.

Recover

25 Tattered Imbuements

Purging or converting a T1 imbuement returns 25 Tattered Imbuements of that perk type.

Imbue

Collect matching tatters → Craft a Scroll of Imbuement → Apply with the Imbue tool.

Three-copy perk cap, including race bonuses.

Tatter Drops

Uncommon Tatter

Roughly 1 in 10 eligible triggers.

Rare Tatter

Roughly 1 in 20 eligible triggers.

Level Scaling

Quantity: 2 to max(3, level / 10).

Tier Roll Odds

The perk is fixed; the tier rolls when applied.

80% T1

15% T2

5% T3

Ascend

Currency

Ascendency Shards

Ascend spends the shards generated by deconstructing unwanted gear.

Item Ceiling

Max Rarity

Every item has a maximum rarity tier. Capped items cannot be promoted further.

Upgrade Step

One Tier

Each successful ascend raises the item's current rarity by one tier.

Cost Preview

Promotion Cost

Weapon and armor detail pages expose each item's promotion value.

Menu

Backpack Items

The Ascend menu lists eligible backpack items with costs, stat totals, and current rolls.

What Changes

Changes

Current Rarity rises one tier. Stat ranges, armor bonuses, and perk eligibility follow the new rarity.

What Stays Fixed

Item identity, Max Rarity, base values, and drop or craft sources.

In Ascend, select a backpack item, review its Promotion Cost, and confirm the upgrade.

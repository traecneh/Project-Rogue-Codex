Purge

Purge or Cleanse

Purge

Removes an applied Special Effect or imbuement. Returns 25 Tattered Imbuements of that effect type.

Cleanse

Removes the Corrupted Innate from corrupted gear, restoring its normal innate.

Recovery Rules

No Tier Refund

The original scroll and tier roll are not returned.

No Item Reset

Rarity, base stats, and the item ceiling stay unchanged.

Epic+ Item

Required to apply a replacement imbuement.

The removed effect is lost. Purge returns 25 tatters, not the original 250-tatter craft cost.

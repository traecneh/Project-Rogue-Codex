Deconstruct

Currency

Shard Conversion

Deconstruct turns gear into Ascendency Shards; higher rarity and level grant more shards.

Value

Inspect Payout

Select an eligible backpack item to see its shard payout before confirming. Weapon and armor details also list deconstruction and promotion values.

Dirty Loot

Half Value

Deconstructing dirty looted items returns half the normal shard value.

Imbuements

T1 Returns

Deconstructing T1 Imbuements from the Craft menu returns 25 Tattered Imbuements.

Finality

No Takebacks

Every deconstruct is final; shards go to your inventory and the original item cannot be recovered.

How to Deconstruct

  1. 1

    Open Deconstruct

    Press [R], then open the Deconstruct tab.

  2. 2

    Inspect payout

    Select a backpack item to preview its shard return.

  3. 3

    Single item

    Click Select, then Decon.

  4. 4

    Deconstruct All

    Three presses, then a fourth click to confirm. Includes every eligible backpack item.

Deconstruction is permanent. Check your backpack before using Deconstruct All.
